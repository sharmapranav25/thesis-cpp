Controller 3: Meta-Sim2 adaptation

A trained autoregressive GRU samples renderer recipes. Gaussian feature-density rewards train it through REINFORCE. Frozen ResNet18 and pixel/edge features; no DINO in training, hybrid initialization, VLM, OCR or target labels.

Paper-based adaptation, not official Meta-Sim2 code. All examples are first draws. Different search budgets and initialization make this a practical comparison, not an isolated algorithm benchmark. These target diagnostics were previously seen in earlier experiments.
