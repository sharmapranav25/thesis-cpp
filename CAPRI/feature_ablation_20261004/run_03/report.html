Simple-hybrid feature utility experiment

Frozen simple neural model; no specialist or XGBoost inference, no training. Positive gains mean lower distance than the simple baseline. Each evaluated variant uses 72 paired fresh-text recipes across three seed blocks. Target families are scored separately.

All 56 style attributes were neutralized individually. Five groups were screened; members of the top two groups were tuned. Fonts are reset to explicit defaults, not literally removed. Font substitutions and overlapping width/slant controls cannot isolate intrinsic attributes perfectly.

Selection uses the existing 60% DINO / 40% image-statistics discrepancy with readability penalties. Independent grayscale/HOG and dark-ink geometry are diagnostic only. Dark-ink geometry includes clutter and is not a validated font metric. Strict readability is a synthetic guard, not OCR accuracy.

City reuses recorded fitting/selection/test images, preserving its last 591 unused images. CryptBB and DarkBay use pixel-disjoint 128/64/256 splits. Policies were frozen before this run's test scoring. Bootstrap intervals are descriptive, uncorrected for many comparisons; seed blocks measure rendering variation, not independent model/search retraining.

These are renderer interventions. They identify candidate controls to prioritize; they do not prove that retraining with fewer heads will improve the feature model. Length and content heads are excluded to preserve paired strings.
