Scope and limitations

Target inputs are pixels only. No target answers, character boxes, masks, texture labels, target fonts or family-specific presets. All target comparisons use predicted masks on both sides. Synthetic labels only supervise perception and check renderer validity.

This MVP searches renderer controls; its update rule is not a trained neural policy. The internal mask encoder was trained on synthetic images and also had auxiliary synthetic attribute objectives. Its attribute predictions and the earlier learned adjustment network are not used here. Real mask accuracy is unknown.

Text length, content group and canvas-size distribution remain the frozen hybrid estimates. The controller can change font, geometry, palettes and effect presence/levels. It cannot guarantee exact target-font identity or arbitrary texture recovery.
