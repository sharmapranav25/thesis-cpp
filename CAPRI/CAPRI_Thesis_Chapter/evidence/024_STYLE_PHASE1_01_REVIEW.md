# Phase 1 pilot 01 review — failed gate

**OBSERVED:** Initial end-to-end pixel measurements, block adjacency/global-line linking, fit-only renderer search and generation completed for five families. Three core tests passed. Generated centroids were nearest their own real validation centroids for 3/5 families (required >=4). All five improved >=20% over the generic procedural baseline in the declared descriptor distance. Candidate and generation failures are retained. The numeric and integrity gates did not pass. No Phase 2 model was started.

**Assistant qualitative review (allowed research assistance, not runtime VLM or ground truth):** Viewed all five galleries, each showing eight references and up to 32 generated images. Exact copies were not expected. CryptBB partially captures varied colors and patch-like backgrounds, but misses dense fine patterns and richer typography. City partially captures light grayscale horizontal structure, but strokes are too sparse/thin and lettering inconsistent. Courier poorly matches: generated blocks and pixel fonts replace reference smooth large text/curved strokes. DarkBay partially captures muted backgrounds and mixed contrast, but misses coherent vertical structure and glyph style. Outlined text poorly matches: generated text is filled and gradients/hollow contours are missing. Only 3/5 are even partial broad matches; thus visual gate also fails. Family differences are visible, but not sufficient for success.

**INFERRED failure mechanisms:** Current component segmentation confuses background with glyphs and fails merged/hollow glyphs. This contaminates palettes and estimated text size. Background mapping treats color variation as blocks too readily; global lines alone do not capture vertical bands, smooth curves or gradients. Fit descriptor distance does not adequately penalize incorrect glyph topology or block hallucination. Foreground contribution checks do not establish readability.

**Next development work:** improve foreground/background separation with local contrast and bidirectional contrast; model background palette independently from text; add topology/gradient/periodicity cues and matching general-purpose functions; reconcile glyph proposals with global projections and family-level uncertainty. Validate each cue with controlled synthetic fixtures. Do not patch behavior by family name, reuse real patches, or modify the gate to pass these samples. The inspected validation examples are now additionally exposed; record revisions as development iterations and use a predeclared disjoint historical reserve for the final comparison, explicitly not pristine overall.
