Objective: a learned target-conditioned controller that changes labelled renderer
recipes, starting from frozen simple-hybrid feature predictions. This is inspired
by Meta-Sim distribution adaptation, not a reproduction of Meta-Sim2's grammar RL.

No target answers, target fonts, copied target pixels, manual family presets, old
manually adjusted recipes, or target-derived synthetic training labels are used.
The new renderer grammar is generic: the same parameter bounds and layers are
available to every family. Real images are used only for fitting/selection and
later held-out evaluation. All generated text comes from a fresh random stream.

Synthetic training: 4096 parameterized schemes, 2 independently sampled strings
and image seeds per scheme. Base/perturbed sibling pairs stay in the same split.
Learn a scheme encoder using parameter, text-mask, and contrastive supervision.
Learn recipe corrections from target/current scheme embeddings and current recipe.
Use learned proposals and mixed categorical/continuous search through the real
renderer. No backpropagation is claimed through the black-box renderer.

Controller controls: 38 continuous controls and 9 categorical controls, covering
font, count, character set, size, width, weight, spacing, rotation, jitter, slant,
background/text/effect HSV palettes, palette variation and assignment, opacity,
blocks, bands, grids, lines, curves, dots, noise, rasterization and border.
Original hybrid weights are frozen. The original hybrid render and the new
grammar initialized from hybrid predictions are both baselines: renderer changes
alone must not be credited as learned-controller improvements.

Component RNG streams are independent so changing background operations does not
advance text/layout RNG draws. Labels are generated from actual glyph masks.

Use prior fitting/selection subsets. Reserve fresh final images by pixel hash:
City 256 from its 591 previously unused images, CryptBB 128 from images unused by
the feature ablation, DarkBay 256 from images unused by that ablation. Do not use
these final images for debugging or policy selection. A final test is scored only
after saved policies and fitting-time diagnostic choices are frozen.

Decent means clear visual family improvement across consecutive, unselected
examples, preserved generated labels, and improvement supported by more than one
appearance measure. A single lower training objective is insufficient. Report
failures and families that do not meet this standard. No OCR improvement claim.
