DINO controller v2 — MVP7

Built and evaluated across five families: 720 diagnostic images and 20 retained configurations, each distinct on a three-image probe. The new objective improves on all five families, but no faithful family-match pass is claimed. City and DarkBay show partial visual improvements. CryptBB and SimpleCaptcha still fail in character count/style; Outlined is mixed and regresses under both older appearance comparators. The old DINO metric improves on three families, and the legacy comparator on four, but neither establishes recognition accuracy. All five selected starts are hybrid; three random-bank profiles survive in the Outlined archive. New controls and a more expressive renderer are available, but the scoring/search combination still selects wrong lengths, character groups and fonts. None of the retained profiles selected square frames.

Same frozen DINO ViT-S/8; no VLM or OCR. Target inputs are RGB pixels only. Added multiscale colour-contrast regions, glyph/stroke/layout/background descriptors and a contrast-collapse penalty. Absolute controls now expose charset, layout, size, width, spacing, weight, bold, slant, size variation and curve. Generic glyph outlines and rotated square frames extend the renderer.

Search accumulates coordinate changes and tries a hybrid bank plus a fresh random bank. Eight fit renders per candidate. Six shortlisted candidates per bank plus the previous DINO profile face two validation batches: 32 target images and 24 fresh synthetic examples each. Rank mean objective plus 0.25 standard deviation, with renderer validity gates.

Freeze controls before diagnostics: 48 fresh synthetic samples per arm, and previously exposed target diagnostic pixels. Larger budget, new controls and new score change together; this is not an equal-budget backbone ablation. The region estimator remains heuristic. Renderer usability is not human legibility. No solver was trained. The legacy cross-check shares handcrafted descriptor code with this update, so it is not fully independent.

Generic synthetic region probe: attention IoU 0.317 → pixel-region IoU 0.368 over 24 samples. This does not measure target segmentation accuracy. Disabled new controls reproduce old-renderer pixels exactly in the regression probe. Blank/blur degradation checks pass. Frames are included in synthetic glyph masks.

Outlined

Mixed result: much larger, more visible letters replace the old tiny cursive fragments; renderer usability rises from 89.6% to 97.9%. However, seven characters, alternating contrast, variable filled fonts and incorrect interference fail to match the hollow target. The new appearance score improves only modestly (0.831 to 0.789); much of the objective gain comes from validity. Original DINO appearance worsens (0.686 to 0.930), and the legacy comparator worsens by 31%. The new outline/frame controls were not selected.
