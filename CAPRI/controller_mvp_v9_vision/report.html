DINOv3 and SAM controller MVPs

Target RGB pixels only. No VLM, OCR, supplied target strings or target masks. Synthetic render labels are saved. Same hybrid recipe initialization and coordinate-search code for both backends.

DINOv3 is prepared and architecture-tested, but cannot be evaluated until approved official ViT-S/16 weights are supplied. No DINOv3 result below is fabricated or substituted with another backbone.

SAM regions are object-mask proposals filtered with a geometry heuristic. They are not verified character masks. DINOv3 uses dense features and a border-background clustering heuristic. This is a small MVP, with only 3 fit renders per candidate; test targets were previously exposed in earlier experiments. No solver-transfer claim. A validation usability gate was added after the City export exposed failures: require 100% complete and at least 90% usable validation renders, otherwise retain the baseline. This is a development revision, not a pristine blind test.
