Controller MVP — iteration 2

Stronger glyph and background structure comparisons, editable synthetic length and layout, font mixtures and palette distributions. Target input remains unlabeled pixels.

The old and new comparators first ranked exactly the same 96 rendered candidates. Expanded search then added 96 candidates and new controls. Thus the paired comparison isolates ranking changes; the expanded result also benefits from additional search.

Fresh final target images: City 128, CryptBB 24, DarkBay 128. Each arm generates 72 examples with paired seeds. CryptBB's remaining fresh test is small. Policies were frozen before test inference. No test-driven reruns or family-specific rendering rules.
